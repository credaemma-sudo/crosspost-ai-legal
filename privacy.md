# CrossPost AI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

## 1. Introduction

CrossPost AI respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how CrossPost AI handles information when you use the application.

## 2. Information We Access

When you connect a social media account, CrossPost AI may access information permitted by the permissions you authorize, such as:

- Basic account information
- Public profile information
- Information about videos or posts needed to provide the application's features
- Authorization tokens required to connect your account

CrossPost AI only requests permissions necessary for supported features.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information accessed through connected accounts may be used to:

- Connect your social media accounts
- Detect eligible new video content
- Distribute your content to platforms you have authorized
- Provide automation and cross-posting features
- Maintain connection status and troubleshoot errors

We do not use your connected account information for purposes unrelated to the application's features.

## 4. User Content

You retain ownership of your content. You are responsible for ensuring that you have the necessary rights and permissions to distribute content through CrossPost AI.

## 5. Third-Party Platforms

CrossPost AI works with third-party social media platforms. Information exchanged with those platforms is also subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.

## 6. Data Security

CrossPost AI takes reasonable measures to protect information used by the application. However, no electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

CrossPost AI does not intend to retain personal information longer than necessary to provide its features. You may disconnect an account from CrossPost AI to stop the application's access to that account.

## 8. Children's Privacy

CrossPost AI is not intended for children who are below the minimum age required to use the supported third-party platforms.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The updated version will be made available through the application or its published website.

## 10. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the CrossPost AI developer through the contact information provided with the application.
